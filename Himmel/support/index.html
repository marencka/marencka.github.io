Common questions

The forecast doesn't load. Himmel needs an internet connection to fetch a new forecast. Offline, it shows the last one it saved if that event hasn't passed yet.

I'm not getting notifications. Check Settings → Notifications → Himmel, and that Himmel is allowed to use your location.

My location is wrong. Himmel uses the location iOS gives it while the app is open. You can also add a place by name.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything Himmel stores lives on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
