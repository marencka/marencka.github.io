Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Himmel ("the app") is made by Alexis Danz. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Himmel has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't sell or share your data or use it to track you.

What Himmel uses

  • Location. If you allow it, Himmel reads your approximate location while you use the app, to forecast the sunrise and sunset where you are. You can instead add places by name.
  • Saved places. The places you add are stored only on your device.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, Himmel schedules notifications on your device about upcoming sunrises and sunsets. They are local notifications, and Himmel sends nothing to push servers.

What leaves your device

To get a forecast, Himmel sends a request to its forecast server at himmel.marencka.com containing the latitude and longitude of the place (rounded to two decimal places, about 1 km) and the place name. The server returns the forecast. To compute it, the server retrieves weather data from the public service Open-Meteo, using that rounded location.

Himmel doesn't ask for your name, email or any account, and the request carries no identifier that ties it to you. As with any web request, the server receives your IP address and may keep short-lived technical logs to operate and secure the service. Those logs aren't used for advertising or profiling, and aren't shared.

To look up a place's time zone or name, Himmel may use Apple's location and geocoding services, which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What Himmel does not do

  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No collection of contacts, photos, or other personal files.

Storage and deletion

Saved places and the most recent forecast are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them. You can turn off location or notifications at any time in Settings → Himmel.

Children

Himmel doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's App Store privacy details.
